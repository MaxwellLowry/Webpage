Methalox Gas Generator

Task and technical lead for a small team building the club's first methalox gas generator, which drives the turbine of a pump-fed engine. Fuel and oxidizer are tapped downstream of the pumps and burned very fuel-rich (O/F around 0.25) to keep turbine inlet temperatures in check. Trade studies picked a single pentad impinging element for manufacturability and mixing, a stainless chamber with heavy methane film cooling, and a decoupled gas-gas torch firing through the injector face to give the fuel-rich mixture a hot core to light from. Every part is designed to be made on a mill, lathe or CNC.
